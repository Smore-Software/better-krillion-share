# Privacy Policy: Share Kit for Krillion

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Share Kit for Krillion is an unofficial browser extension. It adds sharing options to the results screen of the daily game at [krillion.io](https://krillion.io). It is not affiliated with Krillion.

## What it reads

The extension runs only on `https://krillion.io`. When you finish the daily dive, it reads your result from the page and from the game's own storage in your browser. That result is the prompts, your answers, their tiers, your score and the percentile chart. It uses this only to build the share text and image card shown in its panel.

## What it stores

It stores your share preferences: which items to include and which spoiler format to use. They are saved with Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync`. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome copies this setting to your other browsers, as it does for any extension setting. The extension stores nothing else.

## What it sends

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no analytics, tracking or ads. Its only network activity is loading Krillion's own tier images from krillion.io, the same images the results page shows, so it can draw them on the image card.

Text and images leave the page only when you click a button. **Copy** puts them on your clipboard, and **Download** saves them to your computer.
